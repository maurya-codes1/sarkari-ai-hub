- **Duration:** 3 hours per 80-mark theory paper.

### H. HSSLC (Higher Secondary School Leaving Certificate)
- **Streams Offered:** Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities, and Vocational.
- **Compulsory Core:** General English + 1 MIL (Mizo) / Alternative English + 3 Stream Electives (+ optional 6th subject).
- **Marks Scheme:**
  - Science lab subjects: 70 Theory + 30 Practical (Pass: 21 Theory + 9 Practical = 30%).
  - Commerce/Arts non-lab subjects: 80 Theory + 20 Project/IA (Pass: 24 Theory + 6 Internal = 30%).
  - Languages: 100 Theory (Pass: 30%).
- **Duration:** 3 hours per theory paper.
